TeleHex is a Vietnamese Telex keyboard laid out on a 7-column hexagonal grid. The letters follow the familiar QWERTY order, but each key is about 30% wider than on a 10-column keyboard.

Delete, capitals and extra characters are gestures, so the hexagonal grid needs no backspace or shift key.

Used to a 10-column keyboard? Pick the Traditional layout ("Truyền thống"): full 10-column QWERTY with shift and backspace keys, and every gesture and suggestion still works.

ON-DEVICE, NO FULL ACCESS NEEDED
• Telex, suggestions and learned words are processed and stored on your iPhone or iPad. TeleHex has no feature that sends data over the network.
• Typing, suggestions, profiles and text shortcuts all work without Full Access. Full Access is only used for key sounds, haptics, dragging keys on the keyboard, clipboard history and letting the app know it is on.
• No ads, no account, no tracking. Open source (MIT).

GESTURE TYPING
• Swipe up on a letter for a capital, swipe down for the number, symbol or emoji shown under it.
• Swipe left on the space bar to delete; swipe further to delete word by word, with a count of the words first.
• Swipe up on the space bar for caps lock, swipe down to switch to English (no Telex).
• Hold the space bar and drag to move the cursor.
• Two-finger swipe to undo / redo.
• Slide to type: glide across the letters of a syllable or an English word, then pick the suggestion.

SUGGESTIONS WITHOUT AUTOCORRECT
• TeleHex never changes what you typed on its own.
• The six tone variants of the current syllable, one tap away.
• Fixes for neighbouring-key slips and missing or extra keys.
• Next-syllable suggestions learned from your own writing.
• Emoji by keyword.
• English mode completes words from a 50,000-word dictionary and turns on by itself in email, web address and ASCII-only fields.

MAKE IT YOURS
• Hold a key and drag it onto another to swap them, right on the keyboard (needs Full Access), or edit layouts in the app.
• Two built-in layouts, Hexagonal and Traditional. Create your own profiles, edit each key layer and set your own swipe-down character for every key.
• Old or new tone placement (hòa/hoà), text shortcuts, clipboard history (needs Full Access), incognito mode, one-handed mode, keyboard height, light/dark theme, accent colour, haptics and key sounds.
• Import your own language packs for Vietnamese and English suggestions.
• Back up and restore your settings to a single file.

TURN IT ON
Open TeleHex and the welcome screen walks you through it: go to Settings › General › Keyboard › Keyboards › Add New Keyboard, pick TeleHex, then choose the Hexagonal or Traditional layout in the app. The app is in Vietnamese.
