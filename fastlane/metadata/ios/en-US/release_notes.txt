- New "Number row" layout: the Traditional layout with a 1 to 0 row on top. Swipe down on the number row for ! @ # $ %, and on the Q to P row for brackets and punctuation. Pick it on the app's home screen.
- Traditional layout: the space bar moves left, with three small keys on each side.
- English words no longer get Vietnamese tone marks once they cannot be Vietnamese, such as "developer" or "class".
- Fix the marks of the word you just typed: delete the space or tap the end of the word, then type the tone key.
- A marked word that is not valid Vietnamese goes back to the keys you typed, for example "user" instead of "ủe".
- Shortcuts expand right after an opening bracket or quote, for example "(dc" becomes "(được".
