- Better typing: suggestions are ordered and cased more sensibly, shortcuts like "k2" and "@@g" expand, auto-capitalisation only after a full stop and a space ("google.com" stays lowercase), undo removes the whole word, and switching Vietnamese/English mid-word finishes the word first.
- More accurate taps: touching the gap between keys picks the nearest key, touch-offset learning is on by default, and fast two-thumb or three-key rolls no longer drop keys.
- Shift and backspace have their own colour; the return key reads Search, Send, Done… to match the field.
- Drag the space bar to the left edge to delete to the start of the line; the number pad hides "-" and "." in whole-number fields.
